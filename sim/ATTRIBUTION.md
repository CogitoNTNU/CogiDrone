# Sim world data and assets: sources and licences

The Bymarka world (`sim/worlds/bymarka.sdf`, generated by `sim/tools/bymarka/make_world.py`)
is built from open data. The generated models (`sim/models/bymarka_*`) are not in git; the
script downloads the data and rebuilds them. If you publish renders or the generated models,
keep the credits below.

## People

The people are Gazebo Fuel models (OpenRobotics: Rescue Randy, Rescue Randy Sitting, Walking
person, Standing person, Male visitor, Casual female, and the animated "actor" meshes). They are
loaded from fuel.gazebosim.org by URL and not redistributed here. See each model's page on
https://app.gazebosim.org for its licence.

`sim/tools/people/make_people.py` makes 36 recoloured copies (`sim/models/person_*`, git-ignored)
of Walking person, Standing person and Casual female from the local Fuel cache: other clothing
colours, skin tones, hair colours and, for injured people, blood stains on the clothes. They are
built on each machine and not committed.
