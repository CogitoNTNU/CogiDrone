# 3D-printed parts

All parts are generated by `hardware/blender/scripts/make_prints.py` and checked for fit in the
Blender model (`hardware/blender/cogidrone_x500.blend`). Print in **PETG** (or ASA). PLA softens in
the sun.

| File | Qty | What it does | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| `xmplus_pole_case.stl` | 1 | Holds the FrSky XM+ on the right landing-gear pole (16 mm carbon tube). Two guides point the antennas down in a 90° V | 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls. Print with the open pocket facing up, with supports under the saddle |
| `xmplus_pole_lid.stl` | 1 | Lid, with grooves for the two zip ties that hold the whole case to the pole | Flat side down |
| `jetson_spacer_10mm_x4.stl` | 1 (4 spacers) | 10 mm M2.5 spacers between the platform board and the Jetson carrier | 100 % infill |

Three mounts are planned for the Jetson build: the camera, the Jetson (the spacers above) and the
Wi-Fi antennas.

- **D435i mount:** Holybro sells it separately (the dev kit page says so), so print
  `X500 V2-depth-camera-mount.stl` from their [3D-print zip](https://docs.holybro.com/drone-development-kit/px4-development-kit-x500v2/download).
  The D435i uses the 45 mm hole pair ("B") with two short M3 screws; the camera's threads are
  shallow. The zip also has optional landing-gear reinforcements.
- **Wi-Fi antennas:** the dev kit's two PCB antennas are clipped (not glued) to the plastic base
  that comes off when the Jetson hangs under the platform board. Slide each outward and lift, then
  fit them to a printed holder. Not designed yet.

## XM+ pole case

- Fits the XM+ (21.5 × 12 × 3.5 mm) with room for the three solder joints. It replaces the wide heat-shrink you would otherwise wrap the receiver in.
- The cable leaves at the top end. Route it up the pole to the body, then to the Pixhawk RC IN. That's about 25 cm with slack: measure the kit's RC cable, and if it's too short, splice the three wires using a spare kit cable.
- Feed each antenna lead through its guide (Ø2.2 mm channel) so that only the last ~30 mm (the thin active tip) sticks out below the carbon.
- Hold it with two zip ties (≤ 3.6 mm wide) around the pole, the case and the lid, sitting in the lid grooves.
- Position: right landing-gear pole, about 45 % of the way down. See `hardware/blender/scripts/rx_placement.py`: this spot had the clearest view toward the ground (95 % of directions) while staying ~10 cm from the Jetson's USB 3 and Wi-Fi and 27 cm from the SiK radio.

## Jetson spacers

Stack, from the top: M2.5 × 18 screw head → platform board (2 mm) → printed 10 mm spacer → Jetson carrier (1.6 mm) → M2.5 locknut.
The extra height over the kit's 5 mm nylon standoffs clears the M.2 slots and the Wi-Fi card on the
underside of the carrier.
